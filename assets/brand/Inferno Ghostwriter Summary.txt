Inferno Ghostwriter

Positioning:  
The AI Ghostwriter for Technical Minds

Visual identity:
- Inferno colormap: deep indigo/purple → violet → magenta → red → orange → yellow
- Friendly spectral mascot rather than an evil ghost
- Subtle technical charts, documents, code/data motifs
- Warm glowing accents without turning the entire brand into flames
- Clean, modern typography
- Broad enough to appeal to developers, data scientists, analysts, engineers, technical marketers, researchers, and subject-matter experts
I particularly like the ghost + laptop + quill combination because it communicates the product immediately without falling into the generic robot/AI aesthetic.

A strong system would therefore be:

Hero: full spectral ghost + laptop + technical visualizations
Logo: simplified spectral ghost/quill mark
Wordmark: Inferno Ghostwriter
Tagline: The AI Ghostwriter for Technical Minds
