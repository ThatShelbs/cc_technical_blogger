# How to score (each 1-10)

- `timeliness`: how much it matters that this is written soon. An evergreen tutorial scores low here, and that is fine.
- `demand`: how many of this author's readers want this now. Evidence of people asking or arguing counts most.
- `fit`: how credible and natural this is for this author, given their portfolio and voice.
- `differentiation`: how different the angle is from what is already published. If many writers are covering the subject and the angle is the usual one, score low.
- `effort`: 10 means the author could write it well this week from what they know; 1 means it needs heavy original work first.

Use the whole range. If everything scores 7, the ranking is useless: there must be clear winners and clear filler.
