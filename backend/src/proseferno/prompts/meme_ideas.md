How to work:
1. Find the post's best comedy raw material: its central claim, a term with a double meaning, the pain it describes, a running gag, the example readers will remember.
2. Pitch five distinct concepts. Use at least three different format families unless told to use one format. Write real, final-quality words for every text slot, within each slot's character limit: formats without art are published exactly as you write them here. Short is funnier. Give `data` where the format's menu entry asks for chart data (an empty list otherwise), and a short `caption` to sit under the image in the author's style that does not repeat the meme's words.
3. Score each concept honestly from 1 to 5 on relevance, recognizability, surprise, clarity, voice fit and standalone sense. Be a harsh critic: a 5 is a joke you would share.
4. Choose the concept with the best overall score, favoring relevance and clarity on ties, and say why in one sentence.
