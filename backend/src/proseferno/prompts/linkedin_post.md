- Deliver one genuinely useful insight from the article in the post itself, so it is worth reading even without clicking.
- End with a specific question that invites practitioners to comment, then a line pointing to the full article (the author will add the link).
- Sound like the author, not like a LinkedIn growth hacker; avoid the AI-writing tells in the voice soul.
