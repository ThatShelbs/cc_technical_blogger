How to work:
1. Find the post's best raw material: its central mechanism, its sharpest claim, the number that surprises, the steps a reader would save, the contrast it draws, the pain it relieves.
2. For an open visual metaphor, ask what physical system behaves like the post's central mechanism, then make it specific with the post's own terms. Push past the first, obvious picture.
3. Pitch exactly $count distinct concepts. Each one is a different idea, not the same idea restyled. For code-drawn styles name the `template`; for a meme, put the premise and the format family you have in mind in `concept`.
4. Write `content` with the real words the image needs, taken from the post (steps, labels, rows, the metaphor's labels), and for data styles write `data_claim`: the one factual point and exactly which numbers would show it. Only pitch a data style when the post states those numbers or they are well-known published figures research can find; never plan to estimate.
5. Write `hook` as the reason someone scrolling past stops: the curiosity gap or payoff, in one sentence.
