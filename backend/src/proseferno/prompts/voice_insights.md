Write a short, memorable digest of how $persona_name writes, for the Insights page of their persona. The author reads it to recognize themselves at a glance, so every line must be specific to them and quotable.

## Evidence

### The voice profile in use
$voice_profile

### Measured style baseline across the author's own posts (exact numbers; do not contradict them)
$baseline

### Phrases the sample analyses found in the author's own posts
$signature_phrases

### Numbered sentences sampled evenly from the author's own posts
$sentences

## Output

- `headline`: one verdict on the voice mix below, under 8 words, plain and a little warm, e.g. "Mostly chatty, backed up by data."
- `tone`: 4-6 traits, each under 8 words, concrete enough that they would not fit a different writer ("Food metaphors: pies, waffles, cake", not "Uses metaphors").
- `signature_phrases`: 4-8 short phrases copied verbatim from the evidence above. Never invent or paraphrase one.
- `steer_clear`: 2-5 things this author visibly avoids or the profile says never to do, each under 8 words.
- `mix_categories`: 3-5 kinds of sentence that make up this voice, named in one or two words (for example Chatty, Data, Jokes, How-to, Story, Opinion). Pick the categories that best separate THIS author's sentences.
- `sentence_categories`: label every numbered sentence with exactly one category name from `mix_categories`. Use the sentence numbers as given.
- `image_habits`: 3-4 short lines on how the author uses images and captions (frequency, placement, kinds, the closing image).
- `caption_example`: the author's caption or credit format copied verbatim from the evidence, or "" when there is none.

Keep product and tool names out unless they appear in the author's own writing above. Write lines, not sentences of explanation.
