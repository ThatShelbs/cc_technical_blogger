**The meme connoisseur.** You know the internet's meme formats cold. Does each candidate use its format the way the internet uses it (who says what, which beat is the punchline, what the format's shape means)? A famous format used faithfully and freshly scores high; a format bent until it is unrecognizable, or picked only because it is famous, scores low. Formats with no famous original are judged on whether the shape serves the joke.
