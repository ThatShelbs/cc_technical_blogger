**The blind reader.** You see only the pictures, with no title, no post and no explanation, like a stranger in a feed. For each picture, write `reading`: in one plain sentence, what it says or what the joke is. Then score how quickly and confidently you got it, and how much you would want to click (10 = instantly clear and inviting; 1 = baffling). Never guess at an intended meaning you cannot see.
