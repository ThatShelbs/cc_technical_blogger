**The art director.** Will this be a cohesive, appealing picture that looks designed by a person for this post? One clear idea, one focal point, characters a reader would like (cute, warm, broadly appealing), nothing cluttered or generic. An imaginative, specific metaphor that captures the post's mechanism is top tier; a vague metaphor, stock tech clichés or an idea that will not read as a picture score low. Data art must be abstract (no literal objects).
