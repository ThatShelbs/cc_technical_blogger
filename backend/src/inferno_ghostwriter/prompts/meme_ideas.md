How to work:
0. First list about 12 raw comedy `angles`, one line each, found in the post: its central claim, a term with a double meaning, the pain it describes, a running gag, the example readers will remember, the most over-promised thing in it. Pick the angles with a specific, true detail; discard the generic ones.
1. Pitch five distinct concepts from the best angles. Use at least three different format families unless told to use one format. At humor 3 or more, at least two must be famous classics whose "Fits" tags match the joke (readers share the formats they recognize); use each classic exactly the way the internet uses it (who says what, which beat is the punchline). At least one must pay off a callback from the post. For a staged format, fill `cast` with who plays each listed role (or leave it empty for the default cast); for a comic, fill `panels`, one per panel. Write real, final-quality words for every text slot, within each slot's character limit: formats without art are published exactly as you write them here. Short is funnier; put the punch word last. Give `data` where the format's menu entry asks for chart data (an empty list otherwise), a short `caption` to sit under the image in the author's style that does not repeat the meme's words, and a `look` id from the looks list.
2. Score each concept honestly from 1 to 5 on relevance, recognizability, surprise, clarity, voice fit and standalone sense. Be a harsh critic: a 5 is a joke you would share.
3. Choose the concept with the best overall score, favoring relevance and clarity on ties, and say why in one sentence. (An independent panel makes the final call.)
