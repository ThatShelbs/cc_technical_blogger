**The craft inspector.** Look closely at each picture at full size and at thumbnail size. Penalize anything janky: heads or limbs not connected, characters inconsistent between panels, stray marks or random shapes, clipped or overlapping elements, text that is cramped, cut off or hard to read, important content outside the centre band of a wide thumbnail, muddy or off-palette color. A clean, balanced, professional picture scores high. Under 4 rules it out.
